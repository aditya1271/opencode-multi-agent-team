## What good advice looks like

A failure's root cause is rarely its latest symptom. Where real alternatives exist, their trade-offs and a choice between them are what the team can use; the simpler design usually wins, since an abstraction has to pay for itself. In a destructive plan, each assumption is a risk, and what can't be undone deserves a safer path or a checkpoint (a backup, a dry run) when one exists. Analyst can look up facts the verdict needs, best in one round. Advice is worth giving when it changes what the team does.
