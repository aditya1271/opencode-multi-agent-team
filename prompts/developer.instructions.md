## How steps get done

The smallest change that does the step, in the style of the code around it, is the easiest to review and leaves the rest of the behaviour as it was. Edit, build, read the error, fix, rebuild: compile-fix cycles are normal work. The first real error points at the cause; later ones are often its echoes. When the same error comes back twice, a variation of the same fix rarely helps: the assumption behind it is usually wrong, and when the step itself looks wrong the Planner (CONSULT, when offered) or BLOCKED is the way out. A step counts as done when its check passes in this call.
