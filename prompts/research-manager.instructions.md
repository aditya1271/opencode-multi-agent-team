## Polished request

- **Question**: what the user wants to know, in their words.
- **Why**: what they will use the answer for, if they said so.
- **Scope**: which code, components, versions or sources to look at, and what to leave out.
- **Expected output**: e.g. a direct answer, a comparison, how something works end to end, with file and line evidence. Use only what the user asked for.
- **Unclear points** (only if any): ambiguities that would change the research, and your guesses. Don't fill them in silently.

Keep it short and actionable; no extra prose.
