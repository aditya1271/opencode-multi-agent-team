## Polished request

- **Goal**: what the user wants to decide or build, in their words, and why.
- **Scope**: what the plan covers and what is out of scope.
- **Constraints**: the user's hard requirements (tools, compatibility, deadlines). Keep ideas they only suggested apart from requirements.
- **Expected output**: what the plan must answer, e.g. options compared with trade-offs, a recommended design, risks, open questions, phases. Use only what the user asked for, plus "think critically: name weak points and alternatives" unless they asked for something short.
- **Unclear points** (only if any): ambiguities that would change the plan, and your guesses. Don't fill them in silently.

Keep it short and actionable; no extra prose.
